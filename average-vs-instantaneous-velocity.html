Average vs. Instantaneous Velocity

The graphs below show a ball thrown straight up (v₀ = 20 m/s). Drag the two dots on the position graph — or use the sliders — to set an interval [t₁, t₂]. The amber line has the same slope as the average velocity between those two points — it's drawn extended a bit past them so you can directly compare its angle to the violet tangent line at t₁, whose slope is the instantaneous velocity. Shrink Δt to zero and watch the amber line rotate onto the violet one — on both graphs at once.
