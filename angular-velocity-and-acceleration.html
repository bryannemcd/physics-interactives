Which way do ω and α point?

Sign convention: counterclockwise rotation (seen from above) is positive, clockwise rotation is negative.

Right-hand rule: curl the fingers of your right hand in the direction the disk turns; your thumb points along ω. Counterclockwise (seen from above) gives ω pointing up; clockwise gives ω pointing down.
